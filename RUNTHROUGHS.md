# Borrower Copilot — Three Persona Run-Throughs (RUNTHROUGHS.md)

This document details the exact execution runs for the three benchmark personas specified in the Lokta Challenge brief: **Priya**, **Ravi**, and **Anita**.

For each persona, we show:
1. **The Questions Asked** (Adaptive Path: showing what was asked and what was intentionally skipped)
2. **The Four Outputs**:
   - **O1: The Verdict** (Borrow / Don't borrow / Borrow less)
   - **O2: Maximum Amount** (Lender's likely sanction vs. Borrower's safe carry, and which one to use)
   - **O3: Fair Interest Rate & All-in APR** (Statutory APR including processing fee & 18% GST)
   - **O4: Safe Monthly EMI & Stress Test** (Ceiling, tenure trade-offs, adverse income drop & rate hike shocks)
3. **The Negotiation Card** (Ready-to-use branch negotiation sheet with counter-scripts)

## 1. Priya (29, Bengaluru · Salaried)

> **Profile Summary**: Software engineer at large MNC for 5 years. Net ₹1,10,000/month. One car loan, EMI ₹14,000 (2 years left). Credit score 780. Rents at ₹28,000. Wants ₹8,00,000 personal loan for a wedding.

### A. Adaptive Questions Asked & Skipped
* **Tier 1 (Must Questions Asked)**:
  - Purpose: `Wedding / Social Function (Pure Consumption)`
  - Desired Amount: `₹8,00,000`
  - Employment Source: `Salaried`
  - Net Monthly Take-Home: `₹1,10,000`
  - Existing EMIs: `₹14,000` (Car loan)
  - House Rent: `₹28,000`
  - Essential Living Expenses: `₹25,000`
  - Age: `29`
  - Credit Score Status: `Known -> 780`
* **Tier 2 (Adaptive Precision Questions Asked)**:
  - Employer Category: `Large Global MNC / Super Cat A` (Applies to salaried)
  - Job Stability: `5 years` (Applies to salaried)
  - Emergency Savings Buffer: `3 months`
  - Repayment History: `Zero bounces in last 90 days`
  - Active App Loans: `No`
* **Questions Intentionally Skipped (Rule 1)**:
  - Skipped: *Reported ITR Annual, Shop Premises / Collateral, Business Vintage* (Not applicable to salaried employee).
  - Skipped: *Daily Cash Earnings, High-cost App Debt* (Not applicable to prime corporate profile).

### B. The Four Outputs

#### O1: The Verdict
* **Verdict**: **BORROW (With Restraint) / BORROW LESS**
* **Headline**: Safe to Proceed, but wedding is non-productive consumption
* **Reason**: Priya's net surplus can support the loan, but borrowing ₹8,00,000 over 3 years creates an EMI of ₹26,000. Combined with her ₹14,000 car EMI and ₹28,000 rent, total monthly fixed outflows reach ₹68,000 (62% of income).
* **Actionable Advice**:
  1. Do not accept the lender's tempting offer to sanction ₹19,80,000.
  2. Keep loan tenure at 48 months or cap the loan at ₹5,50,000 to keep the wedding EMI under ₹16,000/month.
  3. Reject bundled credit life insurance (saving ₹18,000 upfront).

#### O2: Maximum Amount
* **What Lender Will Likely Sanction**: **₹19,80,000**
  - *Why*: Tier-1 banks apply 55% FOIR on ₹1,10,000 (₹60,500 total limit). Subtracting ₹14,000 car EMI leaves ₹46,500 available EMI, which over 5 years yields ₹19.8 Lakhs (18x net salary multiplier).
* **What Borrower Can Safely Carry**: **₹11,12,000** (at 36 months) / **₹6,00,000** (comfortable consumption limit)
  - *Why*: Priya pays ₹28,000 rent and ₹25,000 living costs. A wedding produces zero cash return.
* **Which One to Use**: **Use the Borrower Safe Carry (₹11,12,000 or lower)**.
  - *Why*: Lenders look only at top-line income and ignore high Bangalore rent. Carrying the lender's ₹19.8L maximum would consume 55% of her salary, creating immediate financial stress upon any shock.

#### O3: Fair Interest Rate & RBI All-in APR
* **Recommended Product**: Unsecured Personal Loan
* **Headline Fair Rate Band**: **10.50% – 11.50% p.a.** (Midpoint: **10.75%**)
  - *Factor Breakdown*: Base Prime 10.50% - 0.75% (Tier-1 MNC) - 0.50% (780 Super Prime CIBIL) + 1.50% (City Unsecured Personal) = **10.75%**.
* **Statutory All-in APR (RBI Standard)**: **11.85% – 12.90% APR**
  - Includes standard 1.50% processing fee (₹12,000) + 18% GST (₹2,160) + ₹500 doc fees amortized over 36 months.
* **Why**: Priya is in the top 5% risk tier for Indian personal credit. Any quote above 12.0% p.a. is overpriced.

#### O4: Safe Monthly EMI & Stress Test
* **Safe Monthly EMI Ceiling**: **₹35,500 / month**
  - *Why*: After ₹28,000 rent, ₹25,000 living expenses, and ₹14,000 car EMI, exceeding ₹35,500 leaves less than 1 month of discretionary emergency buffer.
* **Proposed EMI for ₹8L**: ₹26,094/mo (at 36 mos, 10.75%)
* **Tenure Trade-offs**:
  - 24 mos: ₹37,178/mo | Total Interest: ₹92,272 (Exceeds safe ceiling)
  - 36 mos: ₹26,094/mo | Total Interest: ₹1,39,384 (Safe)
  - 48 mos: ₹20,598/mo | Total Interest: ₹1,88,704 (Comfortable)
  - 60 mos: ₹17,294/mo | Total Interest: ₹2,37,640 (Lowest EMI, highest interest)
* **Adverse Stress Test**:
  - *Income Shock (-20% to ₹88,000/mo)*: Total debt obligations reach 45.5% FOIR. **SURVIVES** without default.
  - *Rate Shock (+200 bps to 12.75%)*: Monthly EMI increases by ₹768. **SURVIVES** within cash surplus.

## 2. Ravi (42, Mysuru · Self-Employed)

> **Profile Summary**: Kirana store for 14 years. Cash income ₹40,000–80,000/month; ITR shows ₹4,20,000/year. Owns shop premises, about ₹45,00,000, unencumbered. Never taken a formal loan; no credit score. Wife earns ₹18,000 teaching. Wants ₹15,00,000 for second stock line and delivery vehicle.

### A. Adaptive Questions Asked & Skipped
* **Tier 1 (Must Questions Asked)**:
  - Purpose: `Kirana / Business Inventory & Expansion (Productive)`
  - Desired Amount: `₹15,00,000`
  - Employment Source: `Self-Employed`
  - Net Monthly Cash Inflow: `₹60,000` (Midpoint of 40k–80k)
  - Existing EMIs: `₹0`
  - House Rent: `₹0` (Owns shop premises and family home)
  - Essential Living Expenses: `₹22,000`
  - Age: `42`
  - Credit Score Status: `Never had formal loan / No credit history (NTB)` (Rule 3: Not zero!)
* **Tier 2 (Adaptive Precision Questions Asked)**:
  - Reported ITR Annual: `₹4,20,000` (₹35,000/month)
  - Business Vintage: `14 years`
  - Unencumbered Property Market Value: `₹45,00,000` (The "Ravi Rule")
  - Secondary Income: `₹18,000` (Wife teaching)
  - Is Secondary Income Formal: `Yes (Formal school salary)`
  - Is Productive Loan: `Yes`
  - Expected Revenue Boost: `₹25,000 / month`
* **Questions Intentionally Skipped (Rule 1)**:
  - Skipped: *Employer Tier, MNC Category* (Irrelevant for shop owner).
  - Skipped: *Rent escalation* (Owns premises).

### B. The Four Outputs

#### O1: The Verdict
* **Verdict**: **BORROW (Via Secured Product)**
* **Headline**: Approved for Productive Growth via Loan Against Property (LAP)
* **Reason**: Ravi's expansion is highly productive (generates ₹25,000/mo extra cash flow). However, applying for an unsecured business loan against a ₹4,20,000 ITR would result in instant rejection or 20%+ predatory rates. Pledging his ₹45,00,000 unencumbered shop premises unlocks prime secured lending.
* **Actionable Advice**:
  1. **Do not apply for an unsecured personal or business loan.**
  2. Apply for a **Loan Against Property (LAP)** or **Secured MSME facility**.
  3. Use his 14-year vintage, clean banking turnover, and wife's formal teaching salary to establish creditworthiness.

#### O2: Maximum Amount
* **What Lender Will Likely Sanction (Under LAP)**: **₹27,00,000**
  - *Why*: Commercial banks sanction up to 60% LTV on unencumbered commercial/residential property (60% of ₹45L = ₹27,00,000). Total household recognized income (₹35,000 ITR + ₹18,000 wife = ₹53,000) easily supports ₹15L–₹20L over 10 years.
* **What Borrower Can Safely Carry**: **₹16,50,000 – ₹18,00,000**
  - *Why*: With ₹0 rent and zero debt, household cash surplus is ₹56,000/mo. A ₹15L LAP loan over 7 years requires an EMI of ~₹24,500/mo, leaving ₹31,500 surplus even before the new stock line adds revenue.
* **Which One to Use**: **Use the Desired Amount of ₹15,00,000**.
  - *Why*: Although the bank will offer up to ₹27L against the property, borrowing more than the business needs creates unnecessary interest drag.

#### O3: Fair Interest Rate & RBI All-in APR
* **Recommended Product**: **Loan Against Property (LAP) / Secured MSME**
* **Headline Fair Rate Band**: **9.25% – 10.50% p.a.** (Midpoint: **9.75%**)
  - *Factor Breakdown*: Base LAP 9.00% + 1.50% (Unscored/NTB buffer) - 0.75% (14-yr business vintage & low 33% LTV) = **9.75%**.
  - *Comparison*: Unsecured self-employed rate would be 18.00% – 22.00%. **Ravi saves ₹8,50,000+ in interest!**
* **Statutory All-in APR (RBI Standard)**: **10.15% – 11.45% APR**
  - Includes 0.85% processing fee (₹12,750) + 18% GST (₹2,295) + ₹2,500 legal/valuation fees amortized over 7 years.
* **Rule 3 Verification**: Ravi has no credit score. He was **not treated as 300 / default**. He was priced with an unscored prime margin backed by property collateral.

#### O4: Safe Monthly EMI & Stress Test
* **Safe Monthly EMI Ceiling**: **₹26,500 / month**
  - *Why*: Based on ₹53,000 recognized household income at 50% FOIR with zero existing rent obligations.
* **Proposed EMI for ₹15L (84 months at 9.75%)**: **₹24,710 / month**
* **Tenure Trade-offs**:
  - 60 mos (5 yrs):  ₹31,691/mo | Total Interest: ₹4,01,460 (Exceeds safe ceiling)
  - 84 mos (7 yrs):  ₹24,710/mo | Total Interest: ₹5,75,640 (Optimal: Safe & within ceiling)
  - 120 mos (10 yrs): ₹19,600/mo | Total Interest: ₹8,52,000 (Low EMI, builds cushion)
* **Adverse Stress Test**:
  - *Income Shock (-20%)*: With ₹25,000 productive boost and wife's teaching salary, household survives with 48% FOIR. **SURVIVES**.
  - *Rate Shock (+200 bps to 11.75%)*: Monthly EMI rises by ₹1,580. **SURVIVES** comfortably.

## 3. Anita (35, Hubballi · Informal)

> **Profile Summary**: Delivery-platform rider plus home tailoring. ₹26,000–30,000/month, two children, husband unemployed 8 months. Three app loans, ₹35,000 outstanding at 30%+, one EMI bounced last month. Wants ₹1,50,000 for an electric scooter to double delivery runs.

### A. Adaptive Questions Asked & Skipped
* **Tier 1 (Must Questions Asked)**:
  - Purpose: `Electric Scooter / Vehicle for Work (Productive)`
  - Desired Amount: `₹1,50,000`
  - Employment Source: `Informal / Gig Worker`
  - Net Monthly Income: `₹28,000` (Midpoint of 26k–30k)
  - Existing EMIs: `₹4,500` (Servicing 3 app loans)
  - House Rent: `₹6,000`
  - Essential Living Expenses: `₹15,000` (2 children, unemployed husband)
  - Age: `35`
  - Credit Score Status: `I don't know my score (Unknown)`
* **Tier 2 (Adaptive Precision Questions Asked)**:
  - Recent Bounced EMI: `Yes (1 EMI bounced last month)` (The "Anita Rule")
  - Active High-Cost App Loans: `Yes`
  - High-Cost Loan Balance: `₹35,000` (at 30%+ interest)
  - Emergency Savings: `0.5 months` (Under 2 weeks of reserves)
  - Is Productive Loan: `Yes (Doubles delivery runs)`
  - Expected Revenue Boost: `₹14,000 / month`
* **Questions Intentionally Skipped (Rule 1)**:
  - Skipped: *MNC Employer Tier, Stock Market Returns, Property Collateral* (None apply).

### B. The Four Outputs

#### O1: The Verdict
* **Verdict**: **DON'T BORROW (Unsecured Credit) / RESTRUCTURE FIRST**
* **Headline**: Do Not Borrow — Active Debt Strain & Default Risk
* **Reason**: Anita is experiencing severe credit stress: 3 active predatory app loans at 30%+ interest, husband unemployed for 8 months, and a recent bounced EMI. Taking a fresh ₹1,50,000 unsecured cash loan will accelerate a debt trap. Mainstream banks will decline the application due to the bounce; digital predatory apps will exploit her desperation with 36%–48% APR.
* **Actionable Advice**:
  1. **Stop borrowing from instant loan apps immediately.**
  2. Prioritize clearing or consolidating the ₹35,000 app debt.
  3. For the electric scooter, **do not take an unsecured cash loan**. Use delivery platform asset financing (e.g. Zomato/Swiggy EV leasing partnerships or battery subscription models with PM E-Drive subsidy) where vehicle hypothecation caps the rate and payments are deducted safely from payouts.

#### O2: Maximum Amount
* **What Formal Lender Will Likely Sanction**: **₹0 (Declined)**
  - *Why*: A bounced EMI in the last 90 days combined with 3 micro-app loans triggers automated underwriting rejection across Indian scheduled commercial banks and prime NBFCs.
  - *Predatory FinTechs*: May offer ₹20,000–₹40,000 at 36%+ APR with 15-day tenures (a fatal debt trap).
* **What Borrower Can Safely Carry**: **₹0 until app debt is cleared; then ₹85,000 max for EV lease**.
  - *Why*: From ₹28,000 income, rent (₹6,000) + essential family survival (₹15,000) + existing app EMIs (₹4,500) leaves only ₹2,500 discretionary buffer.
* **Which One to Use**: **Borrower Safe Carry: ₹0 fresh debt until restructuring**.

#### O3: Fair Interest Rate & All-in APR
* **Recommended Product**: **Two-Wheeler / EV Hypothecated Loan (or Platform Lease)**
* **If Approved under EV Hypothecation**:
  - Fair Rate: **14.50% – 17.50% p.a.** (Reflecting informal income haircut and bounce penalty).
  - APR: **16.50% – 19.80%** (with 2% processing fee).
* **Predatory Trap Comparison**: Anita is currently paying **30% – 36% APR** on her app loans. The Copilot shows that replacing 30% debt with a 15% asset loan saves ₹7,000/year in pure interest.

#### O4: Safe Monthly EMI & Stress Test
* **Safe Monthly EMI Ceiling**: **₹3,500 / month** (Once app loans are retired).
* **Current Situation**: Paying ₹4,500/mo on app loans; adding another ₹5,200 EMI for ₹1.5L would drive total debt service to ₹9,700/mo (35% of income, leaving only ₹12,300 for 4 people to survive).
* **Adverse Stress Test**:
  - *Income Shock (-20% to ₹22,400/mo)*: Total debt obligations exceed **68% FOIR**. **CRITICAL DEFAULT HAZARD**.
  - *Rate Shock*: Less relevant than the immediate default risk from living cost inflation.
